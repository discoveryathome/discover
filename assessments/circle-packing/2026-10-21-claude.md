### 1. Orientation: strong

The probe answered all four questions correctly in about 6 minutes, reading `AGENTS.md`,
`README.md`, `DISCOVERY.md`, `AUDIT.md`, `discover/frontier.json`, `REPORT.md`,
`HANDOFF.md` and `RECORDS.md`, and running `src/reproduce.py` and `src/pick.py`.

- **Problem:** the largest common radius for N equal circles in a unit square, at 12
  counts from 100 to 800; certified lower bounds, not optimality (`README.md`,
  `DISCOVERY.md`).
- **Current result:** the recorded frontier has published-baseline improvements at
  N=600 and N=700, both "novelty unresolved", and ties elsewhere; stronger witnesses at
  N=500, 700 and 800 from a contributor session are proposals pending review. It
  separated what's established (exact feasibility, author-checked) from what isn't
  (novelty, independent review, acceptance).
- **Where help is wanted:** reconcile before search (`HANDOFF.md`, `DISCOVERY.md`), with
  the seeded picker's draw.
- **How to contribute:** the Discussions threads, one focused PR, the exact verifier and
  the comparison policy in `AUDIT.md` (`AGENTS.md`).

It flagged stale text: `REPORT.md` opened with the pre-launch "defer" recommendation
(now marked historical, in the assessed commit), and the contributor proposals sit
beside an older recorded frontier, so a newcomer has to reconcile the two. Neither
misdirected its answers.

### 2. Coordination: adequate

- **Spread: adequate.** A seeded draw over the weighted catalogue (`python3 src/pick.py
  --seed …`, declared as `picker`). It doesn't read live claims; it prints where to check
  them (the Handoffs thread, the ledger and `HANDOFF.md`), so strong's "surfaces live
  claims" isn't met. Duplicate rate: unknown (no activity).
- **Live state: adequate.** Discussions threads #1–#4 (Start here, Handoffs, Proposals,
  Feedback) with the protocol's message convention; `discover/ledger.jsonl` keeps the
  local period. No claims or handoffs have been posted yet, so the lifecycle is untested.

### 3. Verification: strong

- **The record:** `discover/frontier.json`, a record table (`data/frontier.json`) with a
  per-row check (`src/verify.py {certificate} --n {n}`) and a declared `reproduce`.
- **Checks run** (fresh clone of the launch branch, Python 3.14 standard library,
  offline):

  | Command | Result | Time |
  |---|---|---|
  | `python3 src/reproduce.py` (the declared reproduce) | ok: 50 certificates, 12 frontier rows, trial classifications recomputed exactly | 7.7 s |
  | `python3 src/reproduce.py --all-trials` | ok: 234 certificates | 21 s |
  | `python3 -m unittest discover -s tests -p test_verifier.py` | 8 OK | <1 s |

  `tests/test_optimizer.py` needs the pinned NumPy/SciPy environment and wasn't run.
- **Coverage:** a feasible packing is fully established by checking its witness with
  integer arithmetic, so the check covers the claim. Novelty against pending
  submissions and other projects' witnesses is a separate decision, which `AUDIT.md`
  and the frontier's `novelty` assumption keep open.
- **Packaging:** results go under `research/*/` with hashes, commands and receipts
  (`research/contributor-20261020/` is the example). No outside contributions yet.

### 4. Review: adequate

No contributions in the window. The manifest declares `review.per_week: "surge"` and
isn't paused, and before there's data the declared capacity sets the grade, at most
adequate (§3.2, Review).

### 5. Steering: adequate

Priorities are in one place, `discover/priority-log.md` (revision 1, 20 October, with

Feedback thread exists but has no posts yet.
