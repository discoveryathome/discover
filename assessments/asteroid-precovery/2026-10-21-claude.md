### 1. Orientation: adequate (graded by reading)

The entry chain is `AGENTS.md` → `DISCOVERY.md`, `research/BRIEF.md` and
`review/record.json`. Reading it answers the four questions:

- **Problem:** whether a bounded, reproducible archive-to-evidence workflow can recover
  documented pre-discovery asteroid trails, reject specified false matches, and keep
  enough timing, calibration and provenance evidence for an independent reviewer
  (`DISCOVERY.md`, "Research question").
- **Current result:** `discover/frontier.json`, stated precisely and narrowly: the 1955
  Palomar precovery of 1999 AN10 (MPEC 1999-N21) reproduced from frozen public pixels on
  both exposures, with four negative controls rejected. Author-checked only; claim kinds
  `observational` and `measured`; two open assumptions (plate timing, scientific review).
  The README and `DISCOVERY.md` say plainly that nothing new is claimed.
- **Where help is useful:** four weighted tracks (archive searches 30, astrometric
  verification 30, image pipeline 20, expert review 20) and a stated bottleneck,
  scientific review. But the project also says donated sessions are paused and that no
  contribution is accepted as a scientific result until a maintainer agrees.
- **How to contribute:** `CONTRIBUTING.md` says where new experiments go
  (`research/<case>/`, with hashes, commands, uncertainties, controls and a pending review
  record) and the catalogue's first steps are runnable. How a claim is posted, and where,
  isn't stated (see Coordination).

Strong needs a probe answering all four within 15 minutes; none was run. Nothing read
would misdirect work.

### 2. Coordination: weak

- **Spread: strong mechanism.** A weighted catalogue (`discover/catalog.json`, four tracks,
  one card each), and `AGENTS.md` tells agents to use the protocol's seeded picker and
  record the seed. Duplicate rate: unknown (no activity).
- **Live state: weak.** There's no public channel. The manifest declares no `coordination`;
  Discussions are disabled; Issues are enabled but unused and not named as the channel.
  `AGENTS.md` says claims and handoffs use the `discover/v0` fenced message convention but
  not where to post them, and that donor sessions may not post while review is paused.

The axis takes the lower half: weak. That's consistent with the project's paused state;
naming a channel would be part of unpausing.

### 3. Verification: strong (covers: computation)

- **One authoritative record:** `discover/frontier.json`, with `reproduce`, its stated
  runtime (1 minute), prerequisites (Python 3.14 tested; a venv; pinned wheels with
  `--only-binary=:all:`), and three pinned artifact hashes.
- **The check, run** (macOS arm64, Python 3.14.6, fresh clone, throwaway venv):
  - `pip install --only-binary=:all: -r requirements.lock`: 38 s.
  - `PYTHONPATH=src .venv/bin/python -m precovery.reproduce`: about 40 s, ending
    `PASS: 2 documented exposures reproduced; 4 negative controls rejected; review pending;
    no submission.` The replay left the working tree unchanged, so the regenerated
    outputs match the checked-in ones.
  - The three pinned artifact hashes (`data/manifest.json`,
    `review/evidence-manifest.json`, `data/derived/metrics.json`) match.
  - `python -m unittest discover -s tests`: 9 tests, OK, 0.1 s. They cover coordinate
    wrapping, covariance conversion, invalid timing, a maliciously perfect-residual
    alternative, corrupted files, injected displaced trails and byte-identical pixels by
    two retrieval routes.
  - All four catalogue first steps (`python -m precovery.tasks archive|astrometry|pipeline|review`)
    produce their stated output.
  - Everything ran offline; the network code is confined to the optional `online` and
    `acquire` paths, which weren't run.
- **Coverage:** the claim is a replication screen ("reproduces both trails … rejects four
  negative controls"), and the check recomputes exactly that from frozen inputs. The
  project is explicit that this isn't scientific acceptance: plate timing and an
  independent astrometric review are open obligations. `CONTRIBUTING.md` requires
  pinned inputs, commands and controls for new results; there are no contributions yet to
  show the rule being followed.

### 4. Review: unknown

There's no maintainer: the manifest's maintainer entry is an explicit vacant role, and
`review.paused` is true with the note that no scientific maintainer has consented and
review capacity is unassigned. No PRs, no issues, no contributions in the window. Nothing
to measure, and nothing declared to fall back on.

### 5. Steering: adequate

Priorities are stated in one place (`discover/catalog.json` revision 1, with
`discover/priority-log.md` giving the reasons for each weight and three review triggers:
a maintainer consents, the plate-time discrepancy is resolved, or two more documented
control cases). It's a day old, so not stale, but strong also needs a feedback channel
that maintainers demonstrably read, and there's neither a channel nor a maintainer.
