### 1. Orientation: strong

The probe answered all four questions correctly in about a minute, reading `README.md`,
`AGENTS.md`, `DISCOVERY.md`, `QUALIFICATION.md`, `FIRST_TASKS.md`, `EVALUATION.md`,
`CONTRIBUTING.md`, `PARTICIPATION.md`, Discussions #1 and #2, `FIRST_REVIEW.md`,
`DISTRIBUTION.md`, `discover.json`, issue #3, `research/ASSESSMENT.md`,
`discover/frontier.json` and `discover/priority-log.md`.

- **Problem:** cheaper binary64 evaluation of the exponential-integrator functions φ₁,
  φ₂, φ₃ on [−4, 4] within a fixed ULP budget, and whether any workload benefits.
- **Current result:** the inherited φ₃ candidate fails its 16-ULP gate (18 ULP,
  reproduced at high precision); a piecewise Taylor candidate observed at most 2 ULP over
  38,874 evaluations, with an author-derived 4-ULP bound pending review; no demonstrated
  end-to-end speedup; all author-checked, macOS only.
- **Where help is wanted:** four tasks at 25% each (replication, bound audit,
  coefficient workload, portability), and the first-contributor trial in issue #3.
- **How to contribute:** setup, the seeded picker, a claim in Handoffs, a session
  package under `research/`, `verify_candidate.py` and `check.py`, one PR, and what stays
  with the maintainer.

Things it noticed, none of which misdirected the answers: `README.md` never defines φₖ
(it came from `research/ASSESSMENT.md`, a pre-qualification document that is still the
manifest's `brief`); `AGENTS.md` and `DISCOVERY.md` repeat a "current entry point" block
above long historical sections; `AGENTS.md` asks for claims with an expiry, while
`CONTRIBUTING.md` doesn't mention claims and the Handoffs thread gives no claim format.
