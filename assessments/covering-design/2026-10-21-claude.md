Read in that order, the four questions have answers:

- **Problem:** minimize the number of k-blocks covering every t-subset of {1..v}; each
  (v,k,t) is its own record (`DISCOVERY.md`).
- **Current result:** `discover/active-frontier.json`: witnesses give C(48,9,3) ≤ 272
  and C(90,12,3) ≤ 678, below the audited published 273 and 679; author-checked
  proposals awaiting separate review. The original five archive rows stay as
  historical controls (`discover/frontier.json`).

  below 272 and 678, live lower-bound tracing, witness retrieval for six cells,
  independent verification).
- **How to contribute:** `AGENTS.md` (Discussions threads, one focused PR, the verifier
  command, result layout).

What keeps it from strong besides the missing probe: the docs are layered (an original
local pilot, a frontier sprint, the 21 October workflow), so the current answer is
spread over several files, and two lines are stale. `DISCOVERY.md` still says the
proposals "do not open donor intake", and `AGENTS.md` describes task choice with the
hub's `discover pick` while the manifest names the project's own picker
(`tools/pick_task.py`), which the last line of `AGENTS.md` also points to.
