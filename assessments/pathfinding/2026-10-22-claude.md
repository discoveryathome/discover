### 1. Orientation: strong

The probe answered all four questions correctly in a few minutes, reading `README.md`,
`AGENTS.md`, `DISCOVERY.md`, `CONTRIBUTING.md`, `PARTICIPATION.md`, `FIRST_TASKS.md`,
`DISTRIBUTION.md`, both assessments, `EVALUATION.md`, `FIRST_REVIEW.md`, `discover.json`,
the distribution-prep `STATUS.md`, the heads of the incumbents, frontier, priority log
and catalogue, Discussions #1 and #2, and issue #3.

- **Problem:** improving verified multi-agent plans on 4-neighbour Moving AI grids under
  bounded search budgets, with SOC, SOL and makespan kept separate.
- **Current result:** author-checked archives for 20 cases; round two gained 118 SOL
  units on 12 of 12 new cases (plus 4 from two handoff smoke runs); random repair won
  development, with no general new-method advantage; LNS2 reproduced but not a
  matched-objective comparison; tighter pair-conflict lower bounds; no record,
  optimality or state-of-the-art claim.
- **Where help is wanted:** issue #3 (an independent first-contributor trial and scoped
  review), then replication, baseline, headroom and verification at 25 each.
- **How to contribute:** the seeded picker or a `FIRST_TASKS.md` package, setup and the
  quick check, `contribute.py` and `verify_candidate.py`, a session folder, one PR, a
  handoff in Discussion #2, and what stays with the maintainer.

It had to guess one thing, the claim format: `AGENTS.md` says to leave claims in
Discussion #2 (TTL 120 minutes), but no format or example is given. Other things it
noticed, none misdirecting: `entry.brief` and `AGENTS.md` point to `research/ASSESSMENT.md`,
a stub that defers to `research/round2/ASSESSMENT.md`; a distribution paragraph is
repeated verbatim in three files; the replication budget differs between the README
(30,000 nodes) and `FIRST_TASKS.md` (3,000); the dated `STATUS.md` still says Linux is
pending, while CI has since passed on Linux and macOS.

- **The record:** `discover/frontier.json`, author-checked, artifacts bound by hash

  `python tools/check.py`, and `reproduce_full` reruns preparation and the trial in a
  copy.
- **Checks run by the worker** (fresh clone of the launch branch; `uv` venv, Python 3.12):

  | Command | Result | Time |
  |---|---|---|
  | `sh tools/setup.sh` (builds LaCAM3; its ctest 6/6) | OK | 118 s |
  | `python tools/check.py` (`reproduce`) | PASS: 143 route certificates | 64 s |
  | `tools/check_round2.py` | PASS: 349 certificates | 323 s |

  | `contribute.py` (maze, seed 419), then `verify_candidate.py` | valid plan, verified | seconds |

  | hub `discover validate` / `discover screen` | conformant / no findings | seconds |

  After the wording fix (`1caa516`, docs and manifest hashes only), `check.py --quick`,
  pytest and the manifest tamper tests were rerun and pass. CI passed on ubuntu-latest
  and macos-latest.

- **Coverage:** the check recomputes every route certificate (collisions, swaps, goal
  arrival, objective values) and the lower bounds, which is what the archive claims. The
  method comparisons are stated as measured and qualified (development selection, LNS2
  optimizing a different objective), and `reproduce_full` replays them.
- **Packaging:** session folders with candidate, trace, handoff, hashes, seeds and
  limits; frozen evidence is never edited.
- **Sources:** Moving AI maps and scenarios are ODC-By 1.0, with a notice. A build log
