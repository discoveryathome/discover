## Doesn't fit the rubric

- **Same-model rechecks.** The rubric's independence (§9.1, §3.2 Coverage) is about who checks, not which model. A recheck by the same model in a fresh context catches transcription and arithmetic errors, but shares blind spots with the author. Whether it counts toward Coverage should be stated in the spec; this record treats it as not counting.
- **A maintainer's own work as the only activity.** All five PRs are the maintainer's, so Review and the duplicate rate stay unmeasured however much happens. This is correct under the rules.
