### 1. Orientation: strong

The probe answered all four questions correctly in about 6 minutes, reading `AGENTS.md`,
`README.md`, `DISCOVERY.md`, `discover.json`, `discover/frontier.json`,
`results/summary.json`, `discover/ledger.md`, `discover/catalog.json`, the trial report,
a review handoff and the priority log, and running the picker.

- **Problem:** least-mass discrete sizing and topology for the classic 10-bar and 25-bar
  trusses under frozen linear models, with the published reference profile and the
  tube/Euler extension kept separate.
- **Current result:** six author-checked frontier rows (for example 10-bar tube topology
  at 5327.18 lbm; 25-bar reference at 484.33 lbm, 0.148% below the published 485.05). It
  separated reproduced published baselines from wins, and the conditional 13.35% result
  on a separate individually-sized model, "not accepted into the frontier".
- **Where help is wanted:** replication and source audit (40), verification (25), search
  (25), practical transfer (10); first, an independent audit of the models, the benchmark
  transcription and both solvers.
- **How to contribute:** the seeded picker, a claim in the Handoffs thread, one focused
  PR, `scripts/reproduce.py` and `scripts/check.py` in the pinned venv, and a reviewer's
  decision in `discover/ledger.md`.

Stale text it noticed: the opening paragraphs of `discover/ledger.md` and
`discover/priority-log.md` still say intake is paused (dated 2026-10-20, superseded by the
launch entries below them); test counts differ (12 vs 13); catalogue cards say `python`
where the venv's interpreter is needed. None misdirected the answers.

### 3. Verification: adequate (covers: computation, in the frozen models)

- **The record:** `discover/frontier.json`, six rows, with a declared `reproduce`.
- **Checks run** (fresh clone of the launch branch, pinned venv, about 42 s to install):

  | Command | Result | Time |
  |---|---|---|
  | `scripts/reproduce.py` (declared) | PASS: 13 tests, 18 stored results re-solved by both solvers | 1.9 s |
  | `scripts/check.py results/10bar-tube-topology-winner.json` | PASS | 0.7 s |
  | `research/readiness-20261021/check_manifest.py` | PASS, 70 hashes | 0.1 s |
  | feature-trial `validate.py`, `replay_sample.py`, tests | PASS, PASS, 4 OK | 1.5 s, 5.9 s, 2.9 s |

- **Coverage:** every stored design is re-solved by two separately coded solvers, with
  mass, constraints and arrays checked, so the check covers each row's feasibility and
  mass within the frozen float64 models. It doesn't cover optimality or novelty (which
  the record doesn't claim), and both solvers come from one author with no external
  solver cross-check. Adequate. Strong needs an independent or external solver agreeing.
- **Prerequisites:** Python 3.12+ with the pinned NumPy/SciPy/Matplotlib stack in a
  venv, 1 GB RAM, CPU only; declared in `requirements`.
