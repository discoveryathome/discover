### 1. Orientation: strong

The probe answered all four questions correctly in about 8 minutes, reading `AGENTS.md`,
`README.md`, `DISCOVERY.md`, `discover/coordination.json`, `discover/frontier.json`,
`discover.json`, the readiness package's README, the priority log and
`discover/frontier-table.json`.

- **Problem:** 6-bit, equal-amplitude phase settings for a fixed 16-element
  half-wavelength array at 3 GHz that cut weighted off-target power against quantized
  coherent steering, under efficiency, beamwidth and pointing constraints; ideal
  array-factor model, no hardware claim.
- **Current result:** the best feasible witness per held-out mask (about −13.1 to
  −15.0 dB weighted peak upper bound), author-checked, not accepted, and explicitly not
  a global optimum, a literature record or a hardware result. It named what's pending:
  an independent replay, RF/array review of the model and the float64 enclosures, a
  modern phase-only comparator.
- **Where help is wanted:** robust synthesis (55), verification (20), replication (15),
  literature and bounds (10), and the second-cohort masks.
- **How to contribute:** a seeded pick, a claim in the Handoffs thread, one focused PR,
  `python -m src.check` and the tests, and what stays with the maintainer.

The probe couldn't run the picker: it needs the pinned venv (NumPy), which the
requirements and README state but the probe wasn't allowed to install. Stale text it
noticed: `README.md` refers to a `../.venv` outside the repo; test counts differ between
`README.md` (13) and `docs/VERIFICATION.md` (8, a dated receipt). Neither misdirected the
answers.

### 3. Verification: adequate (covers: computation, in the ideal model)

- **The record:** `discover/frontier.json` and `discover/frontier-table.json`, four
  held-out masks, with a declared `reproduce`.
- **Checks run** (fresh clone of the launch branch, pinned venv from
  `requirements.lock.txt`, about 48 s to install):

  | Command | Result | Time |
  |---|---|---|
  | `python -m src.check` (declared) | PASS: 72 witnesses re-evaluated by a separate evaluator and angle grid, 192 stress replays, hashes | 11 s |
  | `python -m pytest -q` | 13 passed | 8 s |
  | `python -m src.check --full-stress` | PASS | 89 s |
  | `python -m trial.check` | PASS | 33 s |

- **Coverage:** the check recomputes every witness and the frontier values from the
  committed phases with an independent evaluator, so it covers the claim as scoped (best
  feasible witness in this set, in the ideal model). But the peak upper bounds are
  float64 curvature enclosures without directed rounding, and beamwidth and pointing are
  numerical, so the bound claims aren't rigorous. Adequate. Strong needs rigorous
  (interval or directed-rounding) enclosures, or the record scoped to numerical values.
- **Prerequisites:** Python with NumPy, SciPy and jsonschema from the lock file, 1 GB
  RAM, CPU only; declared in `requirements`.
