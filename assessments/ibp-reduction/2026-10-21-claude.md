### 1. Orientation: strong

The probe answered all four questions correctly in about 8 minutes, reading `AGENTS.md`,
`DISCOVERY.md`, `README.md`, `discover/frontier.json`, the priority log, the catalogue and
the reports, and running the picker.

- **Problem:** cheaper policies for reducing Feynman integrals to master integrals by
  integration-by-parts, at CPU scale, in exact arithmetic, with cost in field
  operations, across seven one-loop families (`DISCOVERY.md`, `README.md`).
- **Current result:** a learned policy from a frozen 40-candidate search uses a
  geometric mean of 0.3334 of the sparse baseline's field operations on three held-out
  families, author-checked and awaiting review; the contributor trial's "online
  elimination" candidate is a proposal that regresses on one family. It listed what's
  pending: the held-out set is now public, no Kira or FIRE comparison, no multi-loop
  case, basis minimality unaudited.
- **Where help is wanted:** verification (35), production-solver replication (30),
  generalization (25), literature (10), verification and replication first.
- **How to contribute:** the Discussions threads, one focused PR, what a proposal must
  include, and what stays with the maintainer.

Stale text it noticed: the contributor-trial report's closing line (under a Historical
banner), the priority log's revision 1 "Do not open donor intake yet" (superseded by the
launch entry below it), `discover/completion.md` (a pre-launch done message), and
`AGENTS.md` pointing to the long protocol draft for the message format. None misdirected
its answers.

### 3. Verification: strong (covers: computation)

- **The record:** `discover/frontier.json`, with a declared `reproduce`.
- **Checks run** (fresh clone of the launch branch, a scratchpad venv from the pinned
  `requirements.txt`, built in 16 s):

  | Command | Result | Time |
  |---|---|---|
  | `python -m ibp.experiment reproduce` (declared) | PASS: 17 exact certificates; sparse and learned costs reproduced on 7 families | 11 s |
  | `python tools/check_trial.py` | PASS: 16 trial certificates | 38 s |
  | `python tools/check_project.py` | PASS: schemas, hashes, frozen binding | 0.2 s |
  | `python -m unittest discover -s tests` | 15 OK | 6 s |

- **Coverage:** the claim is that each reduction is correct and costs the stated number
  of field operations. The check verifies every reduction as an exact rational-polynomial
  identity and recomputes the operation counts deterministically, so it covers the claim.
  It doesn't cover, and the record doesn't claim: wall-clock or memory, minimality of the
  master bases (an open assumption), or any comparison with Kira or FIRE. The checker
  shares SymPy and the family definitions with the reducer, so an independent
  implementation would strengthen it.
- **Prerequisites:** Python 3.11+, pinned SymPy 1.14.0 and jsonschema, macOS or Linux;
  declared in `requirements` and the README.
