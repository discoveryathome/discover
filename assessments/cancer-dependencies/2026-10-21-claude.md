### 1. Orientation: strong

The probe answered all four questions correctly in about 6 minutes, reading `AGENTS.md`,
`README.md`, `CURRENT.md`, `DISCOVERY.md`, `discover/program.json`, `QUICKSTART.md`, the
pre-release handoff, the broader-expression package's README, `discover.json` and the
catalogue.

- **Problem:** predicting cancer-cell-line dependencies in new biological contexts from
  public DepMap CRISPR screens, well enough to guide experimental prioritization;
  computational hypotheses only, not drug targets or clinical advice.
- **Current result:** the broader-expression trial raised development mean Spearman from
  0.2948 to 0.3471 (author-checked arithmetic), but Broad organoid transfer was 0.1100 vs
  0.1273 for the matched reference: no aggregate transfer gain. MDM2, BRAF and PRMT5 are
  exploratory post hoc leads. Nothing is scientifically accepted.
- **Where help is wanted:** an independent scientific reviewer, an independent replay, a
  reproduced published reference, a prospective MDM2 protocol, an independent evaluation
  set; seven catalogue cards over two tracks.
- **How to contribute:** the Handoffs thread, a seeded pick, a frozen proposal before
  outcomes for exploration, a `research/<attempt>/` package and one focused PR; scientific
  acceptance stays with the maintainer after independent review.

The probe couldn't run the picker (it needs the pinned venv's `jsonschema`, which it
wasn't allowed to install). Stale text it noticed: `reports/PRE_RELEASE_HANDOFF.md` has a
Historical banner but its body still says intake is pending; the pre-release contribution
paragraph is repeated in four files. Neither misdirected the answers.

## Safety screen

`discover screen`: no findings. Scope: public cell-line screens, no human subjects. The
unchanged DepMap `Model.csv` carries the publisher's cell-line donor annotations (age,
sex, race; treatment details for 3 of 2,105 lines), distributed under CC BY 4.0; Sanger
individual IDs are hashed; no patient-level data is committed. The docs state results are
computational hypotheses, not clinical advice, and sessions mustn't fetch
controlled-access or human-subject data. `claims_risk` is the thing to watch: leads like
MDM2 must stay labelled exploratory.
