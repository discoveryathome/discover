### 1. Orientation: strong

The probe answered all four questions correctly in about 6 minutes, reading `AGENTS.md`,
`README.md`, `DISCOVERY.md`, `discover.json`, `discover/frontier.json`, the ledger, the
priority log and the record table, and running the picker.

- **Problem:** maximize the minimum distance of binary linear [n,k] codes at 16 cells
  (n=41–62, k=10–12), seeking a verified construction beating every published and
  automatically implied lower bound.
- **Current result:** no new record. The 16 published lower-bound witnesses are
  reconstructed and checked by two exhaustive implementations, author-checked; the
  contributor trial replicated the published [173,20,68] control and ruled out one- and
  two-column replacements. Upper bounds and classification proofs aren't replayed
  (assumption A1, open).
- **Where help is wanted:** independent replay of the certificates, a literature
  freshness audit, then structured search (weights 25/30/35/10).
- **How to contribute:** the Handoffs thread, one focused PR with a `research/<seed>/`
  package, both verifiers, and what stays with the maintainer.

Stale text it noticed: `docs/assessment.md` (marked historical) still says review
capacity is zero; `review.note` says "author-checked by one agent session" (true, but
reads as pre-launch). Neither misdirected its answers.
