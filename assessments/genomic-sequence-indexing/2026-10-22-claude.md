### 1. Orientation: strong

The probe answered all four questions correctly in a few minutes, reading `README.md`,
`AGENTS.md`, `DISCOVERY.md`, `PARTICIPATION.md`, both assessments, `DISTRIBUTION.md`,
`CONTRIBUTING.md`, `FIRST_TASKS.md`, `EVALUATION.md`, `FIRST_REVIEW.md`, `discover.json`,
`discover/frontier.json`, `discover/priority-log.md`, the distribution-prep `STATUS.md`,
Discussions #1 and #2, and issue #3.

- **Problem:** exact substring counting (both strands) on public bacterial reference
  genomes, comparing a compressed index (Ropebwt3 rank2a), a persisted 8-base lookup
  cache and an uncompressed suffix-array control on throughput, startup and memory.
  Software only.
- **Current result:** all 9,865 method and query comparisons match a direct-count
  oracle; long-query throughput up 19.5–23.5% in confirmation; whole-process time at
  100,000 queries mixed (−14.1% to +16.0%, then +2.7% to +13.7%); about 1 MiB more index.
  Author-checked on one machine; no novelty, production, portable-speedup or break-even
  claim; the panels are now exposed, not held out.
- **Where help is wanted:** issue #3 (an independent first-contributor trial and scoped
  review), then replication, compressed baseline, break-even and workload at 25 each.
- **How to contribute:** setup and the quick check, the seeded picker,
  `contribute.py` and `verify_count.py`, a session folder, one PR, a handoff in
  Discussion #2, and what stays with the maintainer.

It had to guess the claim format: `discover.json` sets a 120-minute claim TTL, but no
document said how to claim. The follow-up commit `0d09671` fixes this: `CONTRIBUTING.md`
now has a "Claiming work" section with a schema-valid example (§6), linked from
`AGENTS.md` and appended to Discussion #2. Other things it noticed, none misdirecting: `AGENTS.md`
line 3 still points first to `research/ASSESSMENT.md` (a stub) and `results/trial.json`
(round one) before saying round two is current; what's pending and where help is wanted
sit at the end of `research/round2/ASSESSMENT.md`; the README says Linux CI results are
available while `DISTRIBUTION.md` and the dated `STATUS.md` say Linux is unclaimed or
pending (CI has since passed on Linux and macOS).
